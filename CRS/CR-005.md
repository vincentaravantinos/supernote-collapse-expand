# CR-005: Investigate registerPluginLifeListener for B-013

**Status:** Analyzed

## Request

`BUGS/B-013.md` ("live icon-drag redraw doesn't survive a full device
reboot") is closed **Won't fix**: two fix attempts (lazy hydration
triggered by the drag gesture; recollapse-on-init) failed on-device, and
the investigation was time-boxed given the narrow severity. The shipped
mechanism is `rehydrateExpandedRegistry`, an eager fire-and-forget
hydration called at plugin init with no way to know when — or whether —
it has actually completed before a drag gesture needs its result.

The Chauvet 3.29.43/2.26.40 release adds
`PluginManager.registerPluginLifeListener` — lifecycle events (init,
mount, start, pause, unmount, destroy), with automatic re-delivery of
the most recent event if you register within 1 second of it firing.
This is a new primitive that didn't exist during B-013's investigation:
it may allow gating drag-redraw readiness on a real lifecycle signal
instead of a blind fire-and-forget race. Not guaranteed to fix the
underlying issue (root cause was never conclusively pinned down), but
changes the diagnostic and design picture enough to be worth a fresh
look rather than leaving this as a permanent documented limitation.

Source: r/Supernote_dev, "Plugin Preview Build: Chauvet 3.29.43 Beta for
Manta / Nomad and Chauvet 2.26.40 Beta for A5X / A6X" (u/Dunn-sn), Add
section.

## Impact analysis

**High impact — escalate to Feature.** Re-engineering the init-hydration
flow around a new, thinly-documented SDK primitive (the 1-second
re-delivery window's exact semantics are unconfirmed) is a design
question, not a mechanical fix, and touches `expandedRegistry.ts` and
every call site gating on it (`iconMoveRedraw.ts`, `expandAction.ts`,
`recollapseAction.ts`). Also carries real risk of re-opening a
previously time-boxed investigation without a conclusive outcome —
worth flagging to the user explicitly during requirements/architect
review rather than assuming success.
