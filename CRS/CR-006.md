# CR-006: Absorb pre-existing content dragged into an expanded section's zone

**Status:** Done

## Request

Clarify and fix the "absorb" semantics for a section's zone while
expanded. Two expected behaviors, confirmed with the user:

1. **Resizing (dragging the icon while expanded) must never absorb
   anything.** If the new, stretched shape happens to cover pre-existing
   content, that content is simply visually hidden beneath the mask —
   left completely untouched at the data level, regardless of why it
   ended up under there.
2. **Recollapse must absorb content that ended up inside the zone
   because the user deliberately put it there after expanding** — not
   just newly-drawn strokes (already specified, SPEC.md's Recollapse
   section), but also pre-existing content from elsewhere on the page
   that the user dragged into the zone after the section was expanded.

Currently, "pre-existing" is tracked purely by whether an element's
`numInPage` existed at expand time (`preservedNums`), captured
without regard to position. This can't distinguish:
- content that was already positioned inside the zone before expand
  (must stay protected, never absorbed — already correctly specified
  and, as far as known, correctly implemented), from
- content that existed elsewhere on the page before expand, then got
  dragged into the zone afterward (per point 2 above, should now be
  absorbed on the next Recollapse — not currently specified, and not
  correctly handled by a purely number-based `preservedNums` check).

Surfaced while investigating B-019 (a stress-test scenario dragged a
native, untracked link into an expanded section's zone, then dragged
the icon again). Not yet established whether fixing this absorb
semantic also resolves B-019's crash — that remains open separately.

## Impact analysis

**High impact — escalate to Feature.** This isn't a mechanical fix:
distinguishing "was already positioned inside the zone at expand time"
from "existed elsewhere and got moved in afterward" requires tracking
*position*, not just presence, which the current data model
(`CollapseSection.preservedNums: number[]`) can't express. Concretely
this needs either:
- storing each preserved element's position (or at minimum, whether it
  was inside the zone) alongside its num at expand time, or
- re-deriving "was it in the zone at expand time" some other way (e.g.
  comparing current position against a recorded zone snapshot) at
  recollapse time instead of expand time.

Either approach is a real data-model change (`CollapseSection`'s shape)
touching `expandOne` (capture), `recollapseOne` (the absorb-scan
decision), and the persistence/crash-safety guarantees SPEC.md's
"Persistence requirements" section already imposes on any new state.
It also needs a new/amended `REQ-NNN` (this is a genuinely new,
independently-testable behavior — "content dragged into the zone after
expand is absorbed on next Recollapse" — not covered by the existing
"new strokes" wording) and a corner-case check for the boundary
question: what happens to a stroke that's *partially* inside the zone,
or that was itself resized/redrawn (not just moved) after expand.

## Next step

Done. Implemented as REQ-200/210/220/230 (SPEC.md) via a zone-scoped,
incrementally-grown `preservedNums` (see DECISIONS.md 2026-09-22).
Confirmed on-device.
