# B-028: Name ends up under the section's content after dragging the icon inward

**Status**: Fixed

## Symptom

Dragging a named, expanded section's icon toward the inside of its area:
the redrawn content partly covers the name.

## Hypothesis

The redraw pushes the icon back out to just outside the zone
(`projectIconOutsideZone`), but moves the name by the raw drag delta
(`iconRect − entry.iconRect`), not by the icon's final position (`iconR`).
The name lands inside the area, under the content.

## Evidence

User report 2026-09-29 (same drag as B-027). Code reading.

## Resolution

Fixed: the name's delta is computed from the icon's final (projected)
position. Confirmed on-device (pen + finger inward drags, outward drag,
handle drag). Introduced in this unreleased cycle — no CHANGES.md entry.
