# B-024: Dragging the CR-008 resize handle triggers a full redraw but the area doesn't actually resize

**Status**: Closed — not a bug, confirmed on-device

## Symptom
While a section is expanded, dragging its bottom-right resize handle
(CR-008): the plugin visibly reacts — selection dismissed, the
"working..." busy popup shown, a full redraw runs — but the section's
area ends up unchanged. No resize is applied.

## Root Cause (confirmed)
The drag was done with a **finger**, not the pencil. `iconMoveRedraw.ts`'s
`onMotionDown`/`onMotionUp` (the icon/handle resize listener) apply no
`toolType`/`pointerCount` filtering at all — unlike `iconTapToggle.ts`'s
`onTapDown`/`onTapUp`, which explicitly restrict tap-to-toggle to
`toolType === 1 && pointerCount === 1` (finger). So a finger drag near
the handle still passes our own in-memory hit-test and tap-distance
check, and the full redraw pipeline runs — but the device doesn't
actually relocate a page element under a finger touch (finger is
reserved for panning/scrolling; only the pencil manipulates ink
elements). The handle reads back at its unchanged position, the
computed area is identical to before, and the redraw is a costly no-op.

Confirmed on-device: the identical drag with the pencil resizes the
area correctly.

## Expected behavior
This was never CR-008 scope. Finger-drag support for the icon/handle
resize controls is tracked separately as BACKLOG item 6, not yet
implemented.

## Resolution
Not a bug in CR-008's resize logic. Closing without a code change here;
BACKLOG item 6 already tracks adding real finger-drag support (and,
per this investigation, now also covers making the resize listener
either honor `toolType` or handle a finger touch as a genuine
move — right now it just silently no-ops instead of recognizing the
gesture won't do anything).
