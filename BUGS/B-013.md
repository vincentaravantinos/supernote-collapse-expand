# B-013: Live icon-drag redraw doesn't survive a full device reboot

**Status**: Won't fix — documented as a known limitation (see CHANGES.md,
README.md). Not a regression from data-safety or collapse/expand/recollapse
correctness — only the live drag-to-reshape redraw is affected.

## Symptom
Expand a section, then **fully reboot the device** (not just a plugin/app
restart) without recollapsing first, then drag the icon — the frame does
not reshape live. Tapping the icon to collapse/expand it works normally the
whole time. A plain process restart (force-stop, or switching apps and
back) does not reproduce this — only a genuine device reboot does.
Rebooting a second time reliably clears it.

## Investigation summary
Extensive on-device tracing (many full-reboot repro attempts, several
logcat capture strategies including a properly-armed disconnect→reconnect
watcher) never conclusively pinned the exact root cause. What's confirmed:
- `expandedRegistry` (the in-memory tracking live-redraw depends on) is
  empty at touch time after a failing reboot, and the motion listener
  itself is registered and firing correctly (`onMotionDown`/`onMotionUp`
  pair with real coordinates).
- The underlying data (`isExpanded`) is read correctly by that point —
  tapping the icon right after a failing reboot still correctly triggers
  Recollapse, which requires the same read to already be correct.

Two fix attempts were implemented and tested on-device, neither resolved
it:
1. **Lazy, flag-gated hydration** triggered by the drag gesture itself
   (instead of an eager fire-and-forget attempt at plugin init) — still did
   nothing. Most likely explanation: a real drag's down→up window can
   complete faster than the async hydration chain resolves, so the result
   arrives after the one moment it was needed.
2. **Recollapse-on-init** (recollapse anything still expanded on the
   current page automatically at plugin init, sidestepping live-redraw
   tracking entirely for the restart case) — also did not visibly
   recollapse the section in the reported test. Whether this was a timing
   issue (retry budget too short) or something else was never conclusively
   determined before the investigation was time-boxed.

Given the cost of further diagnosis (multiple hours, several failed logcat
capture strategies across real device reboots) relative to the severity
(a narrow edge case — an actively expanded section, at the exact moment of
a full reboot, and only the live-drag redraw is affected — normal
collapse/expand/recollapse and the tap shortcut are unaffected), the
decision is to revert to the original restart-survival mechanism (works
reliably for the common case, a plain restart) and document the reboot
case as a known limitation instead of continuing to chase it.

## Resolution
Reverted to the original `rehydrateExpandedRegistry` eager
fire-and-forget-at-init mechanism (build r128). Documented in CHANGES.md's
Unreleased entry and README.md's "Good to know" section.
